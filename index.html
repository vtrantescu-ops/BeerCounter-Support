Support & FAQ

Answers to the questions people usually have after installing Drink Tally. Nothing here requires an account — the app works entirely on your own devices.

What does Drink Tally do?

Drink Tally helps you keep track of how much you're drinking, so you can pace yourself and know when to stop. It keeps the count for the current session visible wherever you are — the app, a Home Screen widget, and your Apple Watch — along with how long the session has run and how long it's been since your last drink.

How do I start counting?

Open the app and tap "Log a Drink". The first tap starts a new session and logs your first drink at the same time — there's no separate "start" step. Every tap after that logs one more.

Can I set a limit?

Yes — turn on "Set a Drink Limit" at the top of the Settings tab and choose a number that's right for you. When you reach it, Drink Tally shows a check-in suggesting you stop, have some water and get home safely, and asks you to confirm before logging anything more, with "Get Me Home" offered first. The widget and Apple Watch app show when you've reached your limit too. With no limit set, the same check-in appears once a session reaches 6 drinks.

What does the widget do?

Add the Drink Tally widget to your Home Screen for a shortcut to the current count. While a session is running, tapping it logs a drink; if nothing is running, it opens the app so you can start one from there.

Does it work on Apple Watch?

Yes — install the companion watch app to see the live count on your wrist and tap to log a drink. It stays in sync with your iPhone automatically over your paired Apple Watch connection; no internet connection is used for this.

What is "Get Me Home"?

A slide-to-confirm control on the main screen that sends you home. By default it opens Uber, with your current location as pickup and your saved home address as drop-off. If you would rather not use Uber — or it does not operate where you are — switch it to Apple Maps transit directions under Settings › Ride Home. Set your home address once from the Settings tab and it is ready whenever you need it.

Please drink responsibly

Drink Tally counts drinks — it does not estimate blood alcohol content, measure impairment, or tell you whether it's safe to drive. Never rely on a tally to make that call. If you've been drinking, use "Get Me Home," call a taxi, or arrange another sober ride instead of driving yourself.

How do I see or delete past sessions?

The History tab lists every past session with its date, duration, drink count and location. Swipe left on any entry to delete it.

Why does Drink Tally ask for my location?

Location is used for two optional things: labeling each session with the place it happened, and setting the pickup point for "Get Me Home." You can decline the permission and still use the counter, the widget, and the Watch app — you'll just lose the location label on sessions and the "Get Me Home" shortcut.

Something isn't working right

Widgets are managed by iOS and occasionally need a nudge: try removing and re-adding the widget, or restarting your iPhone, if a count looks stuck. If a problem keeps happening, note what you were doing right before it and email vt_apps@yahoo.com.

Privacy Policy

The short version: Drink Tally doesn't have a server. There's nothing to sign into and nothing being collected off your device.

Accounts None
Analytics / ads None
Data sold or shared Never
Where your data lives On your devices

What Drink Tally stores

Each session you track — start time, end time, drink count, and an optional location label — is saved directly on your iPhone using Apple's on-device SwiftData storage. This data is shared with the Drink Tally widgets and Apple Watch app only through Apple's App Group and Watch Connectivity frameworks, which keep it moving directly between your own devices. Drink Tally does not run a server, and nothing described here is transmitted to us or to any analytics or advertising service.

Location

If you allow location access, Drink Tally uses it to:

  • Attach a place name to each session (via Apple's geocoding service, which briefly receives your coordinates to resolve them to a place name).
  • Set the pickup point when you use “Get Me Home,” which hands your current coordinates and your saved home address to either Uber or Apple Maps, whichever you have selected under Settings › Ride Home — only at the moment you slide to confirm that action.

Location access is optional. Declining it turns off session location labels and "Get Me Home," and nothing else.

Apple Watch

The count and session state shown on your Watch are relayed from your iPhone using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework, a direct device-to-device link. This never leaves the pair of devices you own.

What we don't do

No account creation, no sign-in with Apple/Google/Facebook, no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising identifiers, no cross-app tracking, and no selling or renting of any data. There is simply no backend for your data to go to.

Third parties

Apple
Location, geocoding, on-device storage, and Watch Connectivity are all provided by iOS/watchOS system frameworks, governed by Apple's own privacy practices.
Uber
Only invoked when you deliberately use “Get Me Home” and Uber is your selected ride option, to open a trip request with your pickup/drop-off coordinates — governed by Uber’s own privacy policy once it opens. Choosing Apple Maps instead keeps that handoff with Apple.

Your choices

Delete any session at any time from the History tab. Deleting the app removes all of its on-device data, including anything shared with the widgets and Watch app, immediately.

Contact

Questions or concerns about this policy or your data: vt_apps@yahoo.com.

Children

Drink Tally is a drink-counting tool intended for adults of legal drinking age in their jurisdiction and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date at the bottom of this page will change with it. Continued use of Drink Tally after an update means you accept the revised policy.
